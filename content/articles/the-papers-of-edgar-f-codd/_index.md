Edgar F. Codd, known as "The Codd Father", or more generally as "The Father of Relational Databases"

<!-- I've now finished WotLK - time to get back into the grind -->

The relational-model papers are ordered by year of publishing.

To find these papers, this page was very helpful: [dblp computer science
bibliography about Edgar F. Codd](https://dblp.org/pid/c/EFCodd.html) (DBLP -
indirectly ACM), as was this one: [Edgar Frank "Ted" Codd >
Publications](https://dl.acm.org/profile/81100425534/publications?Role=author&startPage=0&sortBy=EpubDate_asc)
(ACM), as was this one: [Technical Paper Archive - Research
Reports](https://dominoweb.draco.res.ibm.com/) (IBM)

While reading these, do keep in mind that they are _old_. For example: The first
paper writes about using an index to select the needed domains/columns, a
concept Codd changed his mind on around 1990 - Codd switched to using "column
names" - as he found out some people were creating tables of 100s of columns
wide, which would make it completely infeasable to using indices.

Then also, in his 1988 "Fatal Flaws in SQL" articles, Codd writes about the lack
of uniqueness in SQL. This has been mitigates (though not fixed) by the use of
DISTINCT (introduced in SQL-86) and using PRIMARY KEYs (introduced in SQL-92).

Note: All "RJ[0-9]{3-4}" papers are all from IBM; most papers without link ended
up being included in the book "[The Relational Model for Database Management:
Version 2](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.5555/77708)"
